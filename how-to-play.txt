open-amagami - play Amagami (PS Vita) on Windows from your own copy
=====================================================================

You need your own copy of Amagami ebKore+ for PS Vita (PCSG00291), as a folder
of decrypted files (AMAGAMI.IMG, SCENARIO.CPK, and either eboot.bin or
extracted\eboot_decrypted.elf).
Nothing of the game is in this download.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Run amagami.exe. The launcher opens.
     (If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info, then Run anyway.)
  2. Press "Choose game folder..." and pick the folder of your copy.
     Answer Yes to copy it into the program's own folder.
     Required checks turn green when they are fine; an unlisted version may show a warning.
  3. Press "Start game".

The first time it offers an "Amagami" shortcut on the desktop and in the Start menu
(Settings, "Desktop shortcut" makes them again). Next time, open it and press "Start game".

ENGLISH
  If your copy has the English fan translation (v1.1), the game is in English.
  For a Japanese copy: Settings, "Add English patch...", pick the translation's folder.

CONTROLS
  A gamepad works when plugged in. The mouse works in the menus.
  Keyboard: Z confirm, X cancel, arrows move, Enter start, Backspace select,
            A square, S triangle, Q/W L/R, E/R L2/R2.
  Change the keys in Settings, "Controls...".
  F11 or Alt+Enter: full screen.   Esc: quit.

SAVES AND UPDATES
  Saves and settings: %APPDATA%\open-amagami (Settings, "Open saves folder").
  The launcher says when a new version is out ("Get the new version").
  To update, unpack it over this folder: saves and settings are kept.
  Portable use: put an empty file named portable.txt next to amagami.exe.
